Product Overview

HUAYEE HY480D Mini Skid Steer

The HUAYEE HY480D is the machine that changes the economics of small-site work. At $7,999 with free freight, it delivers a ride-on, sit-down operating experience, a 1,764 lb max lift capacity, and a triple-pump hydraulic system — features you'd expect to pay $14,000+ for in the traditional dealer channel.

The sit-down configuration is a genuine differentiator. Stand-on compact loaders accumulate operator fatigue fast — on a full day of material handling, back and leg fatigue limit productivity. The HY480D's suspension seat reduces operator fatigue and enables longer, more productive shifts. The full canopy provides protection from sun, light rain, and falling debris.

The 3-pump, 3-valve hydraulic system delivers 100 L/min of combined flow, enabling simultaneous boom lift, tilt, and drive without lag or power drop. Most sub-$10,000 compact loaders run a single pump, which forces you to choose between driving and tilting simultaneously. The HY480D runs both at full speed.

At 43 inches wide, the HY480D fits through standard residential gates and barn doorways while delivering a 1,235 lb rated load capacity (800 kg) with a 1,764 lb max lift (tipping load). The universal quick-attach plate is compatible with over 20 attachment types — buckets, pallet forks, augers, brush cutters, snow blades, and more — making this machine as versatile as machines twice its price.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Real answers from our technical team. Call us if your question isn't here.

What engine does the HY480D come with?+
The US market version ships with a 23 HP Briggs & Stratton V-Twin gasoline engine. A Honda engine variant is also available. Both are commercially rated and widely supported by dealers across the US.
What is the difference between rated load and max lift capacity?+
The rated load capacity (1,235 lbs / 560 kg) is the load the machine is designed to carry safely in normal operation. The max lift capacity (1,764 lbs / 800 kg) is the tipping load — the maximum the machine can physically lift before tipping. Working at rated load provides the proper safety margin.
What attachments are compatible?+
The HY480D uses a universal quick-attach plate compatible with standard skid steer attachments from all major manufacturers. This includes buckets, pallet forks, augers, brush cutters, snow blades, grapples, and box blades.
How wide is the machine — will it fit through my gate?+
The machine is 1,087 mm (42.8 inches) wide. Most standard residential and agricultural gates are 48 inches or wider, so the HY480D fits through the majority of gate openings.
Is this a ride-on or stand-on machine?+
Ride-on, sit-down with a suspension seat. This is a key differentiator vs. stand-on compact loaders in the same price range — significantly more comfortable for full-day operation.

We stock machines in Corona, CA (Los Angeles area) and Pennsauken, NJ (Philadelphia area), enabling faster delivery across most of the continental US.
