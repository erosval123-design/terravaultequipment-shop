Product Overview

HUAYEE HY18B Mini Excavator

The HUAYEE HY18B brings Kubota diesel power to the 2-ton compact excavator segment at a price that defies the traditional dealer model. At $13,599 — $2,700 below list price — this machine delivers the engine reliability, digging performance, and operator ergonomics that professional contractors expect from machines costing $25,000 or more.

The Kubota D722 three-cylinder water-cooled diesel engine is the class leader in the sub-2-ton segment. At 14HP, it delivers consistent power, proven reliability, and an EPA-compliant emissions profile. Kubota's reputation for durability means this engine will run tens of thousands of hours with proper maintenance — parts are stocked at Kubota dealers nationwide.

True pilot joystick controls make the HY18B one of the most operator-friendly machines in its class. The dual gear pump (2 × 22 L/min at 20 MPa) delivers smooth, responsive hydraulic flow that experienced operators describe as 'not like the cheaper machines.' The side-swing boom allows the arm to swing independently of the machine's rotation — critical for working close to walls, foundations, and fence lines without repositioning.

At 43 inches wide with a heavy-duty ROPS canopy and adjustable suspension seat, the HY18B is built for full-day professional use. An 85-inch maximum digging depth handles utility trenching, foundation work, and pool excavation. The semi-automatic quick-change coupler and three-way auxiliary hydraulics enable fast attachment changes and compatibility with powered attachments including augers, breakers, and grapples.

What engine is in the HY18B?+
The Kubota D722 — a 3-cylinder, water-cooled diesel engine producing 14HP. This is a genuine commercial-grade Kubota engine, not a clone or off-brand unit. Parts are available at Kubota dealers across the US.
How does the side-swing boom work?+
The boom assembly can swing left and right independently of machine rotation. This allows you to position the arm alongside walls, fences, and foundations without repositioning the tracks — a significant productivity advantage on confined job sites.
What auxiliary attachments can I run?+
The HY18B has 3-way auxiliary hydraulics and a semi-automatic quick-hitch coupler. This enables powered attachments including augers, hydraulic breakers, grapples, tilting buckets, and compaction wheels from standard mini excavator attachment manufacturers.
