Product Overview

BULL 18-X Mini Excavator

The BULL 18-X is the best-selling machine in the BULL lineup — and it's earned that position by outperforming comparably priced excavators from established brands on every metric that matters to working contractors: engine reliability, hydraulic responsiveness, warranty coverage, and total cost of ownership.

At the core is a Kubota D902 three-cylinder water-cooled diesel engine — the same powerplant specified by Takeuchi, Yanmar, and Bobcat across thousands of commercial machines worldwide. At 20HP, it delivers consistent, fuel-efficient output through an 8-hour shift without the fatigue-inducing power surges that plague cheaper single-cylinder alternatives. Parts are stocked at Kubota dealers nationwide, so downtime stays short.

Parker hydraulics translate engine power into pilot controls that feel precise and low-effort from hour one. The dual-speed travel system (1.5 and 2.17 mph) keeps the machine maneuverable in tight quarters and productive on longer site traversals. The adjustable track chassis narrows to 42.9" for gate and fence access, then widens to 52.4" for maximum stability — in under 60 seconds, without tools.

Every BULL 18-X ships with a package valued at $1,390: three digging buckets (8", 16", 40"), a hydraulic ripper, a hydraulic thumb, a quick coupler, and a complete tool kit. Zero tail swing keeps the house within the track footprint in all directions — dig flush against walls, foundations, and retaining structures without repositioning. The 36-month warranty is the most comprehensive coverage available in the 1.8-ton class.

What is included with the machine?+
Every BULL 18-X ships with three digging buckets (8", 16", 40"), a hydraulic ripper, a hydraulic thumb, a quick coupler, and a complete tool kit — a package valued at $1,390.
Do I need a CDL to transport it?+
No. The BULL 18-X weighs 3,925 lbs. Combined with a standard equipment trailer, the total typically stays well under the 26,000 lb CDL threshold. A 3/4-ton pickup truck is recommended for towing.
Is it compatible with third-party attachments?+
Yes. The quick coupler is ISO-standard and compatible with augers, grapples, compaction wheels, rippers, tilting buckets, and more from any manufacturer.
What does the 36-month warranty cover?+
Manufacturing defects in materials and workmanship for up to 36 months from purchase date. Lifetime free technical support is included separately — call or email our US-based engineers for the life of the machine.
How long does delivery take?+
7–15 business days to the lower 48 contiguous states. Freight delivery is included at no charge. Our carrier will contact you to schedule a delivery window.
What engine oil and hydraulic fluid does it use?+
The Kubota D902 uses standard 10W-30 or 15W-40 diesel engine oil. Hydraulic system uses ISO 46 hydraulic oil. Full specifications are in the included service manual.
